      What this prototype is and isn’t

      • It is the asynchronous flow: the question is put to you, you speak, you watch it back, you redo it until you like it — recorded in the browser at full camera resolution, not over a compressed video call.
      • Your own picture fades out while you record. Watching yourself is what pulls a person’s eyes off the lens, so once the take starts the self-view drops away and a target marks where the camera is. Anyone who needs to see themselves can press Show my picture.
      • The checks are mechanical. Voice level, room noise, light and your position in frame are measured from the camera and microphone before and during each take, so a take that will not cut is caught here and not three days later. Position uses the browser’s face detector where there is one and skin tones where there is not — the second is approximate, which is why it warns and never refuses.
      • Not built yet: upload to storage, the host’s recorded clip asking each question (the prototype reads it aloud instead), and the pass that reads your transcript and asks a follow-up when an answer misses the point.
      • Thresholds are placeholders. Voice level, light and length still need calibrating against the reference clips in the Drive folder before any of them should block a take.
